<h3 align="center">Business email on your own domain, with AI apps that can work in it.</h3>

---

xstate gives you email on a domain you own, plus a calendar, file storage and contacts on the same account. It is free for up to 5 people.

- **Email on your domain**: connect a domain and get a working inbox, with IMAP, SMTP and CalDAV for the mail and calendar apps you already use. ([docs](https://xstate.com/docs/mail-apps))
- **AI apps under your rules**: let Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other MCP apps read, draft or send mail, and choose exactly what each one may do. ([docs](https://xstate.com/docs/mcp))
- **Transactional email**: send receipts, alerts and other one-to-one email from your own server with `POST /v1/emails`. ([docs](https://xstate.com/docs/transactional-email))
- **Calendar, files and contacts**: in the same account as your mail.

Read how we protect accounts, mail and files on the [security page](https://xstate.com/docs/security).
